Enhanced Measurement can collect page views, scrolls and outbound clicks. This site also sends app_view and download_click. A download click means the button was clicked; it does not prove the APK finished downloading or installed.

Measurement ID alone cannot let this static admin read private GA4 reports. Fully embedded cross-user live totals require GA4 Data API/OAuth or a server-side analytics backend.
